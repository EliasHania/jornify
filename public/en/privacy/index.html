Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short

  • Jornify helps companies comply with the mandatory daily working time record in Spain.
  • We use your location only at the moment you clock in or out, to check that you're at your workplace. Never in the background.
  • Data is stored on servers in the European Union (Ireland).
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Working time records are kept for 4 years because the law requires it; after that they are deleted.
  • You can delete your account from the app or by email.

About location

The app asks for location permission only while you're using it. When you press a clock-in button, it checks your position once to verify that you're within your workplace's radius and stores it with that entry, together with the distance to the workplace. While the app is open, the map shows your position only on your screen: that position is neither sent nor stored. Jornify does not track your position in the background or outside working hours. If you clock in remotely, the app neither checks nor stores your location. If your company allows clocking in from the web dashboard, the browser asks for your permission and the location is only used when you press the button, just like in the app (it may be less accurate). At the company's kiosk, your location is not used: the entry is linked to the workplace where the kiosk is.

Before asking for permission, the app shows a notice explaining this use, in accordance with art. 90 of Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD). The time of each clock entry is set by the server, not by your phone.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • Recording working time (start and end time of each day) and producing the reports required by law. Basis: the company's legal obligation (art. 6.1.c GDPR and art. 34.9 of the Workers' Statute).
  • Checking that clock entries are made at the workplace, so the record is reliable. Basis: legal obligation and the company's legitimate interest in an accurate record.
  • Managing your account and the service (sign-up, access, password recovery, issues). Basis: performance of the service (art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Security and prevention of misuse. Basis: legitimate interest.

We don't make automated decisions that produce legal effects on you or create profiles for commercial purposes.

4. How long we keep it

  • Working time records, corrections, working terms, absences, shifts, monthly sign-offs, hour bank and Labour Inspectorate accesses: 4 years, as required by art. 34.9 of the Spanish Workers' Statute (terms and absences, 4 years from when they stop being in force). After that period they are deleted automatically.
  • Notifications: read ones are deleted after 6 months and all of them after one year. The push notification identifier, until you log out or leave. The kiosk PIN and badge, until you change them, the company cancels them or you leave the company.
  • Account data: while the account is active.
  • If you delete your account: access is blocked immediately and data that doesn't have to be kept is deleted. If you have no clock entries, everything is deleted. If you do, your entries are kept pseudonymised (your company will see them as “Deleted employee”) and your name, ID number and email are stored separately, blocked, only for the Labour Inspectorate or the courts. They are deleted automatically 4 years after your last clock entry.

5. Who it is shared with

  • Your company (its administrators in Jornify), which is legally required to keep the record.
  • The Spanish Labour and Social Security Inspectorate, through a temporary link your company creates for a specific period (without GPS coordinates), or the courts, when required by law.
  • Your company's legal employee representatives, if any: they see the whole staff's record with personal data pseudonymised (each person appears with a code, without name, ID number, email or location).
  • Providers that help us deliver the service, under a processing agreement and only for that purpose:
    • Supabase: database, authentication and infrastructure. Data is hosted in its European Union region (Ireland).
    • Google: maps (Google Maps) and Google sign-in, if you use it.
    • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.): sending push notifications to your phone, through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging or Apple Push Notification service. They only receive the phone identifier and the title and text of the notification.

These providers are companies based in the USA and could access data from outside the European Economic Area (for example, for technical support). In that case they do so with GDPR safeguards, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising purposes.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability:

  • By writing to eliashania@gmail.com.
  • If you're an employee, also directly with your company, which is the controller of your clock entry data. If your request reaches us, we'll forward it to your company.

We'll reply within one month at the latest. Please note that erasure of working time records is limited by the legal obligation to keep them for 4 years.

If you believe we haven't handled your data properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency at www.aepd.es.

7. Deleting your account

From the app: home screen → user icon → Settings → Delete my account. You can also request it without the app on the Delete account page.

8. Security

Communications between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is restricted by user and by company: each employee only sees their own clock entries and each administrator only those of their company. Passwords are stored encrypted and we never see them.

9. Minors

Jornify is a workplace tool and is not intended for people under 16.
