Delete your Jornify account

Jornify is an app by Hania Digital Labs. You can delete your account and your personal data at any time, from the app or from this page.

Option 1 · From the app

  1. Open Jornify and sign in.
  2. On the home screen, tap the user icon (top right) to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Access is blocked at that moment. If you're the administrator of a company with employees, the app will ask you to dissolve the company before deleting your account.

Option 2 · Without the app

Send us an email from the address you registered with. The subject and text are already filled in:

Request deletion by email

If the button doesn't open your email app, write to eliashania@gmail.com with the subject “Delete Jornify account”. For security, we'll check that the request comes from the account's email address. We process it within 30 days at most.

Deleted
  • Your access to the app (email, password and Google sign-in).
  • Your account data that we have no legal obligation to keep.
  • Your open sessions on all devices.
Kept for 4 years
  • Your working time records (clock entries, hours and location of each entry) and issues, pseudonymised: your company will see them as “Deleted employee”.
  • Your name, ID number (DNI/NIE) and email, stored separately and blocked.

Spanish law requires the company to keep the working time record for 4 years (art. 34.9 of the Workers' Statute). Your identification data is only provided to the Labour Inspectorate or the courts if they request it. It is deleted automatically 4 years after your last clock entry. If you have no clock entries, everything is deleted immediately.
